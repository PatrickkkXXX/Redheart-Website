What we make

Capabilities

Six core lines of work, all fabricated in our own shop from premium-grade aluminium profiles and tempered or frosted glass. Anything outside this list, we will quote as custom.

  • 01

    Aluminium windows

    Sliding, awning and fixed window units in custom sizes, glazed clear, frosted or tinted. Rust-proof frames suited to coastal weather.

  • 02

    Doors & partitions

    Sliding and swing doors, glass partitions and room dividers for homes, offices and commercial interiors where floor space is at a premium.

  • 03

    Wardrobes & closets

    Built-in and freestanding wardrobe systems with mirror or glass inserts, internal shelving and hanging rails laid out to your storage needs.

  • 04

    Kitchen cabinetry

    Overhead and base cabinet runs with glass or louvre panels, drawer banks and sliding doors — templated to the exact run of your kitchen wall.

  • 05

    Display & shelving

    Freestanding and wall-mounted display cabinets and shelving in aluminium and glass, for retail floors, offices and the home.

  • 06

    Custom fabrication

    Window grills, shower enclosures, awnings, canopies and one-off assemblies. Bring a drawing or a photograph and we will quote from it.

Why Redheart

Our standards

We are a working fabrication shop, not a reseller. The people who quote your job are the people who cut, assemble and fit it.

Premium-grade materials
Aluminium profiles and tempered glass selected for coastal conditions — rust-proof, dimensionally stable and finished to hold their colour.
Scheduled turnaround
You receive a fabrication and installation date with your quote, and we work to it. If something moves, you hear it from us first.
Itemised pricing
Quotes are broken down by unit, material and labour. No allowances, no revised figures after the work has started.
Accountable installation
Our own crew installs, levels and cleans up. If a door binds or a panel sits proud, we come back and correct it.

How we work

From measurement to handover

  1. 01

    Site measurement

    We visit, take the dimensions ourselves and talk through how the space is actually used.

  2. 02

    Drawing & quotation

    You receive a layout drawing and an itemised quote covering materials, fabrication and fitting.

  3. 03

    Shop fabrication

    Profiles are cut, assembled and glazed in our shop, where tolerances can be checked before anything ships.

  4. 04

    Installation

    Our crew fits, levels and adjusts on site, then clears the work area before handover.
